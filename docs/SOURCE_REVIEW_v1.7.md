This is a targeted AI-assisted editorial check for new branching exercises and selected legacy audit findings; **not** a claim of comprehensive clinical validation or formal NSW Health endorsement. All patients and investigations are fictional. No actual treatment advice or prescriptions are supplied.

| Topic | Official source | What was compared | Important limitation |
