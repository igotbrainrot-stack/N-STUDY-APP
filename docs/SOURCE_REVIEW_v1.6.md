## Verification rules for further editing

- Keep sources linked to the claim they actually support; acknowledge when national or interstate material is supplementary.
- Do not use generated artwork to infer vitals, ECG traces, blood gases, anatomy or treatment outcomes. Data graphs originate from the authored case numbers.
- Adult-general CERS statements must be qualified by the applicable chart, authorised alterations and local escalation pathway.
- No specific prescribing, dosing, NIV or potassium therapy instructions are provided in these cases. Any future dosing examples require individual evidence and arithmetic validation.
- Repeat source review against current guidelines and obtain qualified clinician verification if used beyond personal study.
