NSW NURSING STUDY LAB — OFFLINE EDITION 1.7
========================================

Windows:
  1. Extract all files to a normal folder.
  2. Double-click START_HERE.bat.
  3. Your default browser opens Nursing_Study_Lab.html.

Mac/Linux:
  Open Nursing_Study_Lab.html directly in a current browser.

The standalone HTML contains all educational content, styles and six
colourful illustrated scenes. No Python, Node.js or online service is
needed to study. External source links need internet. Personal progress,
bookmarks and optional replaced artwork remain in this browser/device.

CONTENT
-------
- 100 core questions across 12 modules and 12 reading guides.
- Two guided three-stage cases.
- Evidence Relay, Trend Detective and a local revision planner.
- Six extensive clinical reasoning rounds / 27 stages, including three
  branching specialty cases, timelines, ISBAR and reading sections.
- New in the 14 November artwork update: six professionally styled
  colourful cartoon case illustrations are embedded for offline use.

ARTWORK
-------
All clinical scene pictures depict fictional patients and are NOT
medical evidence, ECG strips, diagnostic images or treatment instructions.
Case facts and investigation values are written separately in the app.
Each case includes a 'Replace scene artwork' option for a local image,
which is resized and stored in that browser/device. The included images
are available immediately with no import step.

SAFETY / SOURCES
----------------
Personal self-study only. Not accredited NSW Health training, not a
patient-care tool or independently clinically approved reference.
Check all medical facts against current NSW Health policies, your
facility procedures, and appropriate registered clinical reviewers.
The NSW medicines-law framework changed on 5 November 2026; always
verify current directives before using medication knowledge in practice.

Technical source, tests, past clinical audits and a development handover
are in the full GitHub repository (not required to run this offline app).
